# Trilogy outline: *Moonbound*

Series Planner, Part 2 · Draft v1 · Narrator: Tamsin Rook (first person, present tense) · Couple: Tamsin Rook and Senna Ashgrave

**Love scenes** (on the page, at emotional peaks) are marked **[LOVE SCENE]**.

## Trilogy premise

In the Shale Reach, borders are law: a line not walked and chained by a sworn human surveyor every seven years lapses into open ground, where wolves can't shift and can be legally killed. Tamsin Rook, a newly sworn surveyor who is scared of heights and very good with numbers, takes over the route of a surveyor who died on it. Her escort is Senna Ashgrave, the grim heir of a pack that keeps losing ground. When a rockfall mixes their blood, the rare fated bond catches between a human and a wolf, which the treaty punishes with death. Over three books they hide the bond, are torn apart by the war it helps start, and finally rewrite the treaty itself. To do that, Tamsin must give up the chain that made her who she is and become the first human ever sung onto pack ground.

## Book 1: *Chain and Cairn* (Moonbound, Book 1 of 3)

**Summary.** Tamsin has until first snow to re-chain the Ashgrave line, or the pack's oldest forest lapses. Senna is sent to watch her and expects a Registrar's tool. Halfway along the route, a rockfall at the spot where the last surveyor died mixes their blood, and the bond catches. While they hide it from the pack, the Corrie pack and the hunters, Tamsin finds the dead surveyor's hidden field book. It proves the Survey Office has been stealing Ashgrave land on paper. Tamsin files an honest survey at first snow and wins back four miles for Ashgrave.

**Emotional arc.** Distrust, then reluctant respect, then a bond neither of them wanted, then the decision to choose each other anyway. Tamsin learns to trust her own measurements over the Office's map. Senna learns that a human can be on her side.

**Own payoff.** The survey is filed and the land is saved. Tamsin and Senna commit to each other and promise to find a way to make it legal.

**Cliffhanger into Book 2.** A Clause Nine warrant arrives for Tamsin, and it is signed by the witness Odile Marsh, the surveyor everyone believed was dead.

**Chapters**
1. Tamsin arrives in Vellmark by lock barge. In the slate map room, Registrar Penhallow gives her Odile Marsh's route and a deadline of first snow.
2. At the Ashgrave gate Tamsin meets her escort, Senna, who won't shake her hand and assumes she's the Registrar's creature.
3. Day one on the line: Tamsin is terrified of the heights, Senna is contemptuous, and a careless swing of the silver chain burns Senna's wrist.
4. Camp banter by the fire. Tamsin learns that shifting follows the map when she sees Ashgrave wolves stuck in human form on lapsed ground.
5. Someone has freshly re-daubed Cairn Seven's ochre. Corrie scouts led by their heir, Bram Corrie, intercept them, charm Tamsin and needle Senna.
6. Tamsin's chain readings disagree with the Office map by a quarter mile. She keeps quiet, and Senna notices she's hiding something.
7. A storm drives them into a shepherd's bothy, where they talk honestly for the first time: Senna's dying mother, and why Tamsin came.
8. Hunters cross their path. Senna, stuck in human form on lapsed ground, is nearly killed, and Tamsin fights with the chain.
9. A rockfall at the ravine where Odile "died" cuts them both on the same rock. Their blood mixes, and something catches.
10. Tamsin feels Senna's wound as her own and starts to sense which way Senna is. Senna realises what it is and goes cold.
11. Senna tells her about the tether and Clause Nine. They agree to hide it, finish the survey and keep things strictly business.
12. Strictly business fails: the compass sense makes it impossible to be apart. In a cairn, Tamsin finds Odile's hidden field book.
13. Odile's notes prove the Office map is falsified and that Ashgrave has been losing land on paper, not on the ground. Tamsin shows Senna, which is the first real trust between them.
14. At the Ashgrave hold, Maud Ashgrave is ailing but sharp. The pack is hostile to a surveyor, and Tamsin hides the gold ring in her eyes indoors.
15. On the eve of the full moon, Senna has to leave. They kiss for the first time, and then she runs.
16. Full moon: Tamsin watches the pack turn, and her eyes carry the gold ring. Maud sees it and says nothing.
17. Maud summons Tamsin, tells her she knows and warns her, then hands over Odile's last letter: Odile came to Ashgrave before she died.
18. Back on the higher passes, Bram returns with "help" from the Registrar: a second surveyor sent to check Tamsin's work.
19. Tamsin catches the second surveyor tampering with her chain. She and Senna fight about whether to tell the Office the truth or protect the pack.
20. Hunters seize Senna on open ground. Alone, and despite the heights, Tamsin follows the compass sense through the sinkholes after her.
21. Tamsin frees Senna, and in a hot-spring hollow they stop pretending. **[LOVE SCENE]**
22. Tenderness, then the cost: if they're found, both of them die. They choose to go on anyway.
23. The Hollow's exiles shelter them. An elder recognises the gold ring and hints that this isn't the first bond between a human and a wolf.
24. They race to the last cairns before snow. Corrie sings on an Ashgrave cairn to provoke them, and Senna holds back from violence.
25. Tamsin lays the last chain-length as the snow begins. The honest survey restores four miles to Ashgrave.
26. In Vellmark, Tamsin lays Odile's field book and her own survey on the slate floor, and Penhallow is forced to accept them in public.
27. They celebrate, with Senna out of her element in the city. They promise each other they'll find a way to make the bond legal.
28. A Clause Nine warrant for Tamsin arrives, signed by the witness Odile Marsh, who is alive.

## Book 2: *Open Ground* (Moonbound, Book 2 of 3)

**Summary.** Tamsin is arrested and makes Senna run. Penhallow voids every survey Tamsin has signed, and the Ashgrave line lapses at a stroke. The same night Maud dies, and Senna becomes alpha of a pack that can't shift on its own land. Odile reappears with the truth: she is bonded to the leader of the Hollow, faked her death, and signed the warrant to get Tamsin pulled out before she was killed. Tamsin pretends to work for the Office to get back onto the line, Senna believes she's been betrayed, and the packs go to war. When hunters shoot Senna with silver, Tamsin abandons the deal and crosses open ground to find her. Together they re-chain the Ashgrave line under fire, the pack's wolves shift mid-battle, and Penhallow falls.

**Emotional arc.** The bond meets its hardest test: separation, apparent betrayal, and Senna learning to lead while her heart is somewhere else. Tamsin stops protecting Senna with secrets. Senna learns that being alpha doesn't mean being alone. By the end they no longer hide.

**Own payoff.** Penhallow is arrested and the Ashgrave line is restored. Tamsin and Senna stand together under the moon with the gold ring visible to everyone, and they don't hide.

**Cliffhanger into Book 3.** The Surveyor-General arrives from the capital. The Cairn Treaty is annulled, and every border lapses at the spring thaw unless it is re-sworn. The capital's price for re-swearing is to enforce Clause Nine on Tamsin and Senna.

**Chapters**
1. Tamsin is arrested in the map room and makes Senna escape the city while she takes the fall.
2. Penhallow voids every survey Tamsin has signed, and the Ashgrave line lapses. That night at the hold, Maud dies and Senna becomes alpha.
3. In her cell, Tamsin is visited by Odile Marsh, who says, "I signed it to keep you alive," and leaves.
4. Penhallow offers a deal: re-survey the Ashgrave forest for Corrie and the warrant disappears. If she refuses, she loses her chain hand at the next full moon.
5. Senna's first council as alpha: wolves who can't shift on their own land, hunters arriving, and demands for war on Corrie.
6. Tamsin takes the deal, secretly to get back onto the line and closer to Senna.
7. On the line with a Corrie escort, Tamsin can feel Senna's anger through the bond.
8. Senna confronts her in the forest. With Bram watching, Tamsin can't explain, and Senna believes she's been betrayed.
9. Odile turns up on the line with her story: she's bonded to a Hollow wolf, and she faked her death after finding the falsified maps.
10. In the Hollow, Odile's mate Grisel wants the treaty burned. Odile wants it fixed.
11. Odile reveals that the original treaty was sung, and that the Office's written copy cut a clause: ground-right, under which a human could be sung onto pack ground.
12. Tamsin sends Senna a message in the only private language they have: pain in a deliberate pattern through the bond. Senna can't be sure what it means.
13. War: Ashgrave raids Corrie cairns, Corrie retaliates, and hunters flood the open ground.
14. As alpha, Senna is pressed to order a strike that would kill Tamsin's survey party.
15. She calls it off at the last moment. Her council sees weakness, and a challenge starts to brew.
16. Full moon on open ground: for the first time Ashgrave stays human under the moon, and hunters attack the hold.
17. A silver arrow wounds Senna. Tamsin feels it from miles away, breaks the deal and leaves the Corrie party.
18. Tamsin crosses open ground alone, following the compass sense to Senna, hidden in the sinkholes.
19. They reunite, tell each other the truth and forgive each other. **[LOVE SCENE]**
20. They learn the hunters' arrows are tipped with Office chain-silver: Penhallow armed them.
21. The plan: re-chain the Ashgrave line under fire. A voided surveyor needs a second sworn witness, and that has to be Odile.
22. Senna's cousin Rhoswen challenges her for the pack. Senna wins by refusing to fight to the death.
23. The night survey begins, with Tamsin and Odile chaining, Ashgrave wolves guarding and the Hollow joining them.
24. Battle at the cairns. When Bram sees Penhallow's hunters firing on everyone, he turns Corrie against them.
25. Tamsin lays the last link, the land returns, and Ashgrave shifts mid-battle and wins.
26. Penhallow's own clerks arrest him. Under the waning moon, Tamsin and Senna stand together with the gold ring showing.
27. The Surveyor-General arrives: the treaty is annulled, all borders lapse at the thaw, and the price of re-swearing is Clause Nine.

## Book 3: *The Ninth Clause* (Moonbound, Book 3 of 3)

**Summary.** The thaw is ninety days away. Surveyor-General Hester Vane, Tamsin's old teacher, isn't corrupt; she truly believes in a neutral survey. She offers Tamsin a way out: renounce Senna and testify that Senna alone broke Clause Nine. Senna secretly offers to give up her place as alpha and surrender. They refuse to let each other sacrifice alone, and they go after the lost ground-right clause, piecing together the sung treaty one pack verse at a time. The clause exists, but there's a price. A human may be sung onto pack ground only if she walks its bounds alone and lays down her sworn chain forever. After a public trial, Tamsin walks the Ashgrave bounds at the thaw full moon with no chain, through open ground and hunters. All three packs and the Hollow sing her onto the land. Clause Nine is struck, and they marry.

**Emotional arc.** From each trying to sacrifice herself for the other, to choosing together. Tamsin's hardest choice is giving up the identity she built her life on. She does it for herself, because she wants this life and not because Senna needs it. Senna learns to let her.

**Payoff (series ending).** Clause Nine is struck, ground-right is restored, and Tamsin becomes the first human with pack ground. Tamsin and Senna marry, and the new map carved in Vellmark's slate floor shows Tamsin's name on Ashgrave ground. Happily ever after.

**Chapters**
1. Winter at the Ashgrave hold. The capital's ultimatum stands, with ninety days until the thaw.
2. Surveyor-General Hester Vane, Tamsin's old teacher, is principled, cool and not corrupt.
3. Vane offers Tamsin a way out: renounce Senna and testify that Senna alone broke Clause Nine.
4. Senna secretly offers to give up her place as alpha and surrender to the capital, to save Tamsin and the treaty.
5. Each finds out what the other has offered, and they fight: both are trying to sacrifice for the other.
6. They agree that no one sacrifices alone. They'll prove ground-right exists.
7. Only fragments of the sung treaty survive, scattered through each pack's songs.
8. In Corrie, Bram becomes a reluctant ally and gives them Corrie's verse.
9. The Hollow's verse needs Grisel, who still wants the treaty dead.
10. Odile and Grisel's history comes out. Odile convinces Grisel that a fixed treaty would give the Hollow legal ground too.
11. Capital troops and hunters harass them. Senna is hurt, and the shared pain drops Tamsin in the middle of a negotiation.
12. Snowed in at the bothy from Book 1, they have a night of tenderness and something close to a proposal. **[LOVE SCENE]**
13. The verses come together into the ground-right clause: a human who walks pack bounds alone may be sung onto them, but she must lay down her sworn chain forever.
14. The hardest choice: to strike Clause Nine, Tamsin must stop being a surveyor, the thing she has built her life on.
15. Tamsin hunts for another way. Vane says the capital will only accept the old song after a public test.
16. Tamsin asks herself who she is without the chain, and Senna refuses to let her give it up for her sake.
17. Tamsin decides for herself, not for Senna: she wants the Reach, the pack and this life.
18. The trial in Vellmark's map room, with Vane presiding and Penhallow testifying against them.
19. Bram and Odile testify, and the falsified maps are laid on the slate floor.
20. The test is set: at the thaw full moon, Tamsin must walk the Ashgrave bounds without a chain, through open ground and hunters.
21. The walk begins. It must be walked alone, so Senna can reach her only through the compass sense.
22. A sinkhole collapses, and Tamsin has to take the cliff path she has feared since Book 1.
23. Penhallow's hunters close in. The packs sing from every cairn and keep them off without breaking the rule of the walk.
24. Tamsin reaches the last cairn at moonrise and lays down her chain.
25. The three packs and the Hollow sing her onto the ground, and she feels the land claim her. Vane strikes Clause Nine.
26. They marry at the larch cairns and spend their first night as wives. **[LOVE SCENE]**
27. Epilogue, a year later: Tamsin teaches the first class of human and wolf surveyors, Senna leads the pack, and the new map in the slate floor carries Tamsin's name on Ashgrave ground.
