# Trilogy outline: *Moonbound*

Series Planner, Part 2 · Draft v1 · Narrator: Cass Reed (first person, present tense) · Couple: Cass Reed and Mara

**Love scenes** (on the page, at emotional peaks) are marked **[LOVE SCENE]**.

## Trilogy premise

In the Reach, borders are law: a line not walked and chained by a sworn human surveyor every seven years lapses into open ground, where wolves can't shift and can be legally killed. Cass Reed, a newly sworn surveyor who is scared of heights and very good with numbers, takes over the route of a surveyor who died on it. Her escort is Mara, the grim heir of a pack that keeps losing ground. When a rockfall mixes their blood, the rare fated bond catches between a human and a wolf, which the treaty punishes with death. Over three books they hide the bond, are torn apart by the war it helps start, and finally rewrite the treaty itself. To do that, Cass must give up the chain that made her who she is and become the first human ever sung onto pack ground.

## Book 1: *Chain and Cairn* (Moonbound, Book 1 of 3)

**Summary.** Cass has until first snow to re-chain the Highwood line, or the pack's oldest forest lapses. Mara is sent to watch her and expects a Registrar's tool. Halfway along the route, a rockfall at the spot where the last surveyor died mixes their blood, and the bond catches. While they hide it from the pack, the River pack and the hunters, Cass finds the dead surveyor's hidden field book. It proves the Survey Office has been stealing Highwood land on paper. Cass files an honest survey at first snow and wins back four miles for Highwood.

**Emotional arc.** Distrust, then reluctant respect, then a bond neither of them wanted, then the decision to choose each other anyway. Cass learns to trust her own measurements over the Office's map. Mara learns that a human can be on her side.

**Own payoff.** The survey is filed and the land is saved. Cass and Mara commit to each other and promise to find a way to make it legal.

**Cliffhanger into Book 2.** A Clause Nine warrant arrives for Cass, and it is signed by the witness June, the surveyor everyone believed was dead.

**Chapters**
1. Cass arrives in Stonebridge by lock barge. In the slate map room, Registrar Voss gives her June's route and a deadline of first snow.
2. At the Highwood gate Cass meets her escort, Mara, who won't shake her hand and assumes she's the Registrar's creature.
3. Day one on the line: Cass is terrified of the heights, Mara is contemptuous, and a careless swing of the silver chain burns Mara's wrist.
4. Camp banter by the fire. Cass learns that shifting follows the map when she sees Highwood wolves stuck in human form on lapsed ground.
5. Someone has freshly re-daubed Cairn Seven's ochre. River scouts led by their heir, Theo, intercept them, charm Cass and needle Mara.
6. Cass's chain readings disagree with the Office map by a quarter mile. She keeps quiet, and Mara notices she's hiding something.
7. A storm drives them into a shepherd's bothy, where they talk honestly for the first time: Mara's dying mother, and why Cass came.
8. Hunters cross their path. Mara, stuck in human form on lapsed ground, is nearly killed, and Cass fights with the chain.
9. A rockfall at the ravine where June "died" cuts them both on the same rock. Their blood mixes, and something catches.
10. Cass feels Mara's wound as her own and starts to sense which way Mara is. Mara realises what it is and goes cold.
11. Mara tells her about the tether and Clause Nine. They agree to hide it, finish the survey and keep things strictly business.
12. Strictly business fails: the compass sense makes it impossible to be apart. In a cairn, Cass finds June's hidden field book.
13. June's notes prove the Office map is falsified and that Highwood has been losing land on paper, not on the ground. Cass shows Mara, which is the first real trust between them.
14. At the Highwood hold, Ruth is ailing but sharp. The pack is hostile to a surveyor, and Cass hides the gold ring in her eyes indoors.
15. On the eve of the full moon, Mara has to leave. They kiss for the first time, and then she runs.
16. Full moon: Cass watches the pack turn, and her eyes carry the gold ring. Ruth sees it and says nothing.
17. Ruth summons Cass, tells her she knows and warns her, then hands over June's last letter: June came to Highwood before she died.
18. Back on the higher passes, Theo returns with "help" from the Registrar: a second surveyor sent to check Cass's work.
19. Cass catches the second surveyor tampering with her chain. She and Mara fight about whether to tell the Office the truth or protect the pack.
20. Hunters seize Mara on open ground. Alone, and despite the heights, Cass follows the compass sense through the sinkholes after her.
21. Cass frees Mara, and in a hot-spring hollow they stop pretending. **[LOVE SCENE]**
22. Tenderness, then the cost: if they're found, both of them die. They choose to go on anyway.
23. The Hollow's exiles shelter them. An elder recognises the gold ring and hints that this isn't the first bond between a human and a wolf.
24. They race to the last cairns before snow. The River pack sings on a Highwood cairn to provoke them, and Mara holds back from violence.
25. Cass lays the last chain-length as the snow begins. The honest survey restores four miles to Highwood.
26. In Stonebridge, Cass lays June's field book and her own survey on the slate floor, and Voss is forced to accept them in public.
27. They celebrate, with Mara out of her element in the city. They promise each other they'll find a way to make the bond legal.
28. A Clause Nine warrant for Cass arrives, signed by the witness June, who is alive.

## Book 2: *Open Ground* (Moonbound, Book 2 of 3)

**Summary.** Cass is arrested and makes Mara run. Voss voids every survey Cass has signed, and the Highwood line lapses at a stroke. The same night Ruth dies, and Mara becomes alpha of a pack that can't shift on its own land. June reappears with the truth: she is bonded to the leader of the Hollow, faked her death, and signed the warrant to get Cass pulled out before she was killed. Cass pretends to work for the Office to get back onto the line, Mara believes she's been betrayed, and the packs go to war. When hunters shoot Mara with silver, Cass abandons the deal and crosses open ground to find her. Together they re-chain the Highwood line under fire, the pack's wolves shift mid-battle, and Voss falls.

**Emotional arc.** The bond meets its hardest test: separation, apparent betrayal, and Mara learning to lead while her heart is somewhere else. Cass stops protecting Mara with secrets. Mara learns that being alpha doesn't mean being alone. By the end they no longer hide.

**Own payoff.** Voss is arrested and the Highwood line is restored. Cass and Mara stand together under the moon with the gold ring visible to everyone, and they don't hide.

**Cliffhanger into Book 3.** The Surveyor-General arrives from the capital. The Cairn Treaty is annulled, and every border lapses at the spring thaw unless it is re-sworn. The capital's price for re-swearing is to enforce Clause Nine on Cass and Mara.

**Chapters**
1. Cass is arrested in the map room and makes Mara escape the city while she takes the fall.
2. Voss voids every survey Cass has signed, and the Highwood line lapses. That night at the hold, Ruth dies and Mara becomes alpha.
3. In her cell, Cass is visited by June, who says, "I signed it to keep you alive," and leaves.
4. Voss offers a deal: re-survey the Highwood forest for the River pack and the warrant disappears. If she refuses, she loses her chain hand at the next full moon.
5. Mara's first council as alpha: wolves who can't shift on their own land, hunters arriving, and demands for war on the River pack.
6. Cass takes the deal, secretly to get back onto the line and closer to Mara.
7. On the line with a River escort, Cass can feel Mara's anger through the bond.
8. Mara confronts her in the forest. With Theo watching, Cass can't explain, and Mara believes she's been betrayed.
9. June turns up on the line with her story: she's bonded to a Hollow wolf, and she faked her death after finding the falsified maps.
10. In the Hollow, June's mate Rhea wants the treaty burned. June wants it fixed.
11. June reveals that the original treaty was sung, and that the Office's written copy cut a clause: ground-right, under which a human could be sung onto pack ground.
12. Cass sends Mara a message in the only private language they have: pain in a deliberate pattern through the bond. Mara can't be sure what it means.
13. War: Highwood raids River cairns, River retaliates, and hunters flood the open ground.
14. As alpha, Mara is pressed to order a strike that would kill Cass's survey party.
15. She calls it off at the last moment. Her council sees weakness, and a challenge starts to brew.
16. Full moon on open ground: for the first time Highwood stays human under the moon, and hunters attack the hold.
17. A silver arrow wounds Mara. Cass feels it from miles away, breaks the deal and leaves the River party.
18. Cass crosses open ground alone, following the compass sense to Mara, hidden in the sinkholes.
19. They reunite, tell each other the truth and forgive each other. **[LOVE SCENE]**
20. They learn the hunters' arrows are tipped with Office chain-silver: Voss armed them.
21. The plan: re-chain the Highwood line under fire. A voided surveyor needs a second sworn witness, and that has to be June.
22. Mara's cousin Lena challenges her for the pack. Mara wins by refusing to fight to the death.
23. The night survey begins, with Cass and June chaining, Highwood wolves guarding and the Hollow joining them.
24. Battle at the cairns. When Theo sees Voss's hunters firing on everyone, he turns the River pack against them.
25. Cass lays the last link, the land returns, and Highwood shifts mid-battle and wins.
26. Voss's own clerks arrest him. Under the waning moon, Cass and Mara stand together with the gold ring showing.
27. The Surveyor-General arrives: the treaty is annulled, all borders lapse at the thaw, and the price of re-swearing is Clause Nine.

## Book 3: *The Ninth Clause* (Moonbound, Book 3 of 3)

**Summary.** The thaw is ninety days away. Surveyor-General Agnes, Cass's old teacher, isn't corrupt; she truly believes in a neutral survey. She offers Cass a way out: renounce Mara and testify that Mara alone broke Clause Nine. Mara secretly offers to give up her place as alpha and surrender. They refuse to let each other sacrifice alone, and they go after the lost ground-right clause, piecing together the sung treaty one pack verse at a time. The clause exists, but there's a price. A human may be sung onto pack ground only if she walks its bounds alone and lays down her sworn chain forever. After a public trial, Cass walks the Highwood bounds at the thaw full moon with no chain, through open ground and hunters. All three packs and the Hollow sing her onto the land. Clause Nine is struck, and they marry.

**Emotional arc.** From each trying to sacrifice herself for the other, to choosing together. Cass's hardest choice is giving up the identity she built her life on. She does it for herself, because she wants this life and not because Mara needs it. Mara learns to let her.

**Payoff (series ending).** Clause Nine is struck, ground-right is restored, and Cass becomes the first human with pack ground. Cass and Mara marry, and the new map carved in Stonebridge's slate floor shows Cass's name on Highwood ground. Happily ever after.

**Chapters**
1. Winter at the Highwood hold. The capital's ultimatum stands, with ninety days until the thaw.
2. Surveyor-General Agnes, Cass's old teacher, is principled, cool and not corrupt.
3. Agnes offers Cass a way out: renounce Mara and testify that Mara alone broke Clause Nine.
4. Mara secretly offers to give up her place as alpha and surrender to the capital, to save Cass and the treaty.
5. Each finds out what the other has offered, and they fight: both are trying to sacrifice for the other.
6. They agree that no one sacrifices alone. They'll prove ground-right exists.
7. Only fragments of the sung treaty survive, scattered through each pack's songs.
8. Among the River pack, Theo becomes a reluctant ally and gives them the River verse.
9. The Hollow's verse needs Rhea, who still wants the treaty dead.
10. June and Rhea's history comes out. June convinces Rhea that a fixed treaty would give the Hollow legal ground too.
11. Capital troops and hunters harass them. Mara is hurt, and the shared pain drops Cass in the middle of a negotiation.
12. Snowed in at the bothy from Book 1, they have a night of tenderness and something close to a proposal. **[LOVE SCENE]**
13. The verses come together into the ground-right clause: a human who walks pack bounds alone may be sung onto them, but she must lay down her sworn chain forever.
14. The hardest choice: to strike Clause Nine, Cass must stop being a surveyor, the thing she has built her life on.
15. Cass hunts for another way. Agnes says the capital will only accept the old song after a public test.
16. Cass asks herself who she is without the chain, and Mara refuses to let her give it up for her sake.
17. Cass decides for herself, not for Mara: she wants the Reach, the pack and this life.
18. The trial in Stonebridge's map room, with Agnes presiding and Voss testifying against them.
19. Theo and June testify, and the falsified maps are laid on the slate floor.
20. The test is set: at the thaw full moon, Cass must walk the Highwood bounds without a chain, through open ground and hunters.
21. The walk begins. It must be walked alone, so Mara can reach her only through the compass sense.
22. A sinkhole collapses, and Cass has to take the cliff path she has feared since Book 1.
23. Voss's hunters close in. The packs sing from every cairn and keep them off without breaking the rule of the walk.
24. Cass reaches the last cairn at moonrise and lays down her chain.
25. The three packs and the Hollow sing her onto the ground, and she feels the land claim her. Agnes strikes Clause Nine.
26. They marry at the larch cairns and spend their first night as wives. **[LOVE SCENE]**
27. Epilogue, a year later: Cass teaches the first class of human and wolf surveyors, Mara leads the pack, and the new map in the slate floor carries Cass's name on Highwood ground.
