# Chosen concept: A, *Moonbound*

Chosen by: Aashrita · 2026-10-28

- **Hook:** A human surveyor hired to redraw a disputed border discovers that the pack heir sent to stop her is her fated mate, and the treaty she's enforcing makes their bond a capital crime.
- **Trope stack:** fated mates, enemies to lovers, forbidden, forced proximity (wilderness survey), grumpy/sunshine, protective alpha heroine.
- **World idea:** mountain borderlands where three packs and a human city keep a fragile peace by mapping and policing every border. The bond is real magic with costs: shared pain, and scent that can't be hidden.
- **Arc:** Book 1, the bond snaps and they hide it. Book 2, the treaty breaks and they're on opposite sides of a war. Book 3, they rewrite the law together and she claims a place in the pack.
- **Heat level:** slow burn, one or two fully on-page love scenes per book at emotional peaks (from the voice guide; not yet confirmed).
- **Narrator:** the surveyor (the newcomer), first person, present tense, all three books.
