# Fact sheet for Book 1, chapter 2 (from the Continuity Keeper)

**Beat (outline):** At the Highwood gate Cass meets her escort, Mara, who won't shake her hand and assumes she's the Registrar's creature.

**Use the voice sample.** The approved voice sample is this scene. Build the chapter out from it and keep its key lines. Don't contradict it.

**Characters present**
- **Cass:** morning after ch1. Slept badly after the wolf song. She has the missing page 41 on her mind and the half-shifted arm on the cart in her memory. Nervous about heights. Carrying the chain over her shoulder, the satchel and the instrument case (theodolite).
- **Mara:** first appearance. Tall, black braid, amber eyes, bow and bow-callused fingers, claw scar at the collarbone (it may be hidden by her collar). Bitter, watchful, short sentences. Assumes Voss sent Cass.

**Must-include facts**
- Cass hands over (or tries to hand over) Voss's sealed letter. What's in it is a choice for the Drafter, but it must not reveal the falsified map.
- First snow: the innkeeper's "three weeks or tomorrow" line from the voice sample should be set up at the inn this morning, or kept as hearsay.
- June's body was never recovered (Cass: "She didn't come back at all").
- The approach to the gate: two larches a cart's width apart, and a cairn freshly daubed red. Iron-tasting air or water as they enter Highwood land.

**Threads to keep warm**
- Page 41 (Cass shouldn't mention it to Mara yet).
- June's notes called her escort "sullen". Mara's reaction to June's name should hint she knew her.

**Don't**
- Don't reveal shifting on the page yet (that's ch4).
- No burn from the chain yet (that's ch3).
