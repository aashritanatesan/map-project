WORLD RULES
- Seven-Year Chain: a border counts only if a sworn human surveyor walked and chained it within 7 years, or it lapses to open ground where anyone may hunt, wolves included. -> Puts Tamsin's job and Senna's pack's survival on the same line, with a deadline that keeps them together.
- Shifting follows the map: wolves fully shift only on their pack's treaty ground. Elsewhere they're stuck human or half-shifted. -> Senna is vulnerable whenever they cross lapsed or foreign ground.
- Full moon at home: on their own ground at full moon, wolves can't stay human. -> Senna has to leave Tamsin every month, and the tell shows.
- Silver-linked chain: 66 ft, every tenth link silver. Wolves can't touch it bare-handed. -> Tamsin's tool and badge literally burns Senna.
- Sung borders: packs claim land by singing at cairns, and the Office records the songs. A song on the wrong cairn is a declaration of war.
- The tether: a rare fated bond that catches through scent plus blood (one shared wound). It's unbreakable except by death. -> Ties them together against the law.
- Tether effects: shared pain; compass sense (each always knows the other's direction). -> They can't stay apart when the other is in danger.
- The tell: a thin gold ring at the iris edge under moonlight. It can be hidden indoors or in daylight, but never at full moon. -> A monthly risk of exposure.
- Clause Nine: no wolf may bond with, marry or bed a sworn surveyor. Death for the wolf; the surveyor loses her chain hand and every survey she signed is voided. It has never been invoked. -> The capital crime at the centre of the trilogy.

CHARACTERS
Tamsin Rook (narrator): 27. Brown curly hair cropped at the jaw, grey eyes, short and sturdy, ink-stained fingers. Lowland-born, newly sworn surveyor, first posting in the Reach. Wants: to prove she's a real surveyor and to do honest work. Fears: heights, being someone's tool. Speech: warm, wry, rambles when nervous, quotes exact numbers, talks to her instruments. Current state: just arrived, eager and out of her depth. Relationships: none in the Reach yet.
Senna Ashgrave: 30. Tall and lean, black hair in a single long braid, amber eyes, bow-callused fingers, a faded claw scar across her collarbone. Ashgrave heir. Wants: to stop her pack losing ground before her mother dies. Fears: being the alpha who loses the forest. Speech: short sentences, dry, says nothing she doesn't mean. Current state: bitter and watchful; expects Tamsin to be the Registrar's creature. Relationships: Maud (mother), Rhoswen (cousin).
Maud Ashgrave: 64. White braid, a coughing sickness, sharp-eyed. Alpha of Ashgrave. Ailing.
Aldous Penhallow: 55. Neat, spectacles, ink-free hands. Registrar of the Survey Office. Courteous, ledger-obsessed and secretly corrupt: works for the mining guild to make Ashgrave land lapse.
Odile Marsh: 48. Previous surveyor of the Ashgrave line. Believed dead in a rockfall last spring. (Secret: alive, bonded to Grisel of the Hollow; revealed at Book 1 ch28 and Book 2 ch9.)
Bram Corrie: 29. Red-blond, easy smile. Corrie heir, charming, untrustworthy, allied with Vellmark.
Grisel: Hollow leader, Odile's mate. Wants the treaty torn up. (First appears Book 2.)
Rhoswen Ashgrave: Senna's cousin. Challenges her for the pack in Book 2.
Hester Vane: Surveyor-General in the lowland capital, Tamsin's old teacher. Principled, believes in a neutral survey. (First appears Book 3.)

LOCATIONS
The Shale Reach: high slate ridges, larch forest (gold in autumn, needle-litter underfoot), sinkholes that open overnight, sulphur hot springs in the hollows, thin cold air above the tree line. Iron-tasting spring water on Ashgrave land.
Vellmark: a lock city stacked up a gorge, with sluices and ledger halls. The Survey Office is at the top. Controlled by the Office and the mining guild.
The map room: the Survey Office's great hall, with a slate floor carved with the whole Reach.
The Ashgrave line: Odile's old route through the high larch forest. Ochre-painted cairns, numbered. Includes the ravine where Odile "died".
Ashgrave hold: the pack's seat in the high forest. (Details to be set in B1 ch14.)
Corrie valleys: river valleys held by Corrie; furs, trade, safe passage.
The Hollow: sinkhole country with no legal ground; exiles and half-shifted wolves.
The bothy: a shepherd's stone hut on the line (B1 ch7, B3 ch12).
