STORY BIBLE v2 (updated after Book 1, chapter 1)

WORLD RULES
- Seven-Year Chain: a border counts only if a sworn human surveyor walked and chained it within 7 years, or it lapses to open ground where anyone may hunt, wolves included. -> Puts Cass's job and Mara's pack's survival on the same line, with a deadline that keeps them together.
- Shifting follows the map: wolves fully shift only on their pack's treaty ground. Elsewhere they're stuck human or half-shifted. -> Mara is vulnerable whenever they cross lapsed or foreign ground.
- Full moon at home: on their own ground at full moon, wolves can't stay human. -> Mara has to leave Cass every month, and the tell shows.
- Silver-linked chain: 66 ft, every tenth link silver. Wolves can't touch it bare-handed. -> Cass's tool and badge literally burns Mara.
- Sung borders: packs claim land by singing at cairns, and the Office records the songs. A song on the wrong cairn is a declaration of war.
- The tether: a rare fated bond that catches through scent plus blood (one shared wound). It's unbreakable except by death. -> Ties them together against the law.
- Tether effects: shared pain; compass sense (each always knows the other's direction). -> They can't stay apart when the other is in danger.
- The tell: a thin gold ring at the iris edge under moonlight. It can be hidden indoors or in daylight, but never at full moon. -> A monthly risk of exposure.
- Clause Nine: no wolf may bond with, marry or bed a sworn surveyor. Death for the wolf; the surveyor loses her chain hand and every survey she signed is voided. It has never been invoked. -> The capital crime at the centre of the trilogy.

CHARACTERS
Cass Reed (narrator): 27. Brown curly hair cropped at the jaw, grey eyes, short and sturdy, ink-stained fingers. Lowland-born, newly sworn surveyor, first posting in the Reach. Wants: to prove she's a real surveyor and to do honest work. Fears: heights, being someone's tool. Speech: warm, wry, rambles when nervous, quotes exact numbers, talks to her instruments. Current state: just arrived, eager and out of her depth. Trained six years in the lowland capital; first posting. Counts things compulsively (locks, stairs, cairns). Polite to rude people out of habit (her mother). Carries an oath card. Current state (end B1 ch1): curious, uneasy, determined, a little thrilled. Relationships: none in the Reach yet.
Mara: 30. Tall and lean, black hair in a single long braid, amber eyes, bow-callused fingers, a faded claw scar across her collarbone. Highwood heir. Wants: to stop her pack losing ground before her mother dies. Fears: being the alpha who loses the forest. Speech: short sentences, dry, says nothing she doesn't mean. Current state: bitter and watchful; expects Cass to be the Registrar's creature. Relationships: Ruth (mother), Lena (cousin).
Ruth: 64. White braid, a coughing sickness, sharp-eyed. Alpha of Highwood. Ailing.
Voss: 55 ("in his fifties"). Neat, grey coat and waistcoat, thin silver-rimmed spectacles, polished black shoes, ink-free hands (Cass notices this first). Knows the floor map so well he never looks down. Registrar of the Survey Office. Courteous, ledger-obsessed and secretly corrupt: works for the mining guild to make Highwood land lapse.
June Marsh: 48. Previous surveyor of the Highwood line. Small, slanted, precise handwriting; margin asides in her notes. Officially died in a rockfall in the ravine below Cairn Eleven, late spring; her chain and crushed instrument case were found, her body never recovered. Her notes mention a "sullen" escort. (Secret: alive, bonded to Rhea of the Hollow; revealed at Book 1 ch28 and Book 2 ch9.)
Theo: 29. Red-blond, easy smile. River heir, charming, untrustworthy, allied with Stonebridge.
Rhea: Hollow leader, June's mate. Wants the treaty torn up. (First appears Book 2.)
Lena: Mara's cousin. Challenges her for the pack in Book 2.
Agnes Bell: Cass's instructor in the lowland capital (B1 ch1, "has never made a joke in her life"). Becomes Surveyor-General by Book 3. Cass's old teacher. Principled, believes in a neutral survey. (First appears Book 3.)

LOCATIONS
The Reach: high slate ridges, larch forest (gold in autumn, needle-litter underfoot), sinkholes that open overnight, sulphur hot springs in the hollows, thin cold air above the tree line. Iron-tasting spring water on Highwood land.
Stonebridge: a lock city stacked up a gorge. Fourteen locks from the gorge bottom to the city; 212 stairs from the top lock to the Survey Office. Water runs everywhere (weirs in streets, narrow bridges). Smells of wet slate, coal smoke, larch. Controlled by the Office and the mining guild.
The Survey Office: dressed grey slate, tall many-paned windows overlooking the Reach; corridor lined floor to ceiling with ledgers going back a century.
The inn at the top lock: Cass's room has a slanted ceiling, a sagging bed, and a window facing the Highwood ridge.
The map room: chapel-sized; the whole floor is the Reach carved in slate, hills in relief, rivers as channels with real trickling water, borders painted in red ochre with cairn numbers. NO BOOTS ON THE MAP: shoes left on a bench outside the double doors; people walk it in socks. Cold underfoot.
The Highwood line: June's old route along the high ridge. 31 numbered cairns, ochre-painted. The ravine is below Cairn Eleven. On the floor map, the section between Cairns Nine and Fourteen has been recut (paler slate, sharper groove) and bends about a quarter mile inward into the forest; Voss says it was amended for the ravine collapse.
Highwood hold: the pack's seat in the high forest. (Details to be set in B1 ch14.)
River valleys: river valleys held by River; furs, trade, safe passage.
The Hollow: sinkhole country with no legal ground; exiles and half-shifted wolves.
The bothy: a shepherd's stone hut on the line (B1 ch7, B3 ch12).

ESTABLISHED FACTS (running list)
- ch1: The surveyor's chain is 66 ft, 100 links, every tenth link silver.
- ch1: Wolves on open ground can only half-shift; the hunter's kill on the cart was half-shifted (man's arm furred grey from the elbow, clawed hand), tagged like a parcel.
- ch1: Voss's stated Clause Nine wording: "No wolf of any pack shall bond, marry, or lie with a sworn surveyor of the Reach. The wolf shall be put to death. The surveyor shall lose the hand that holds the chain, and every survey she has signed shall be void." Clause Nine has never been invoked.
- ch1: The Highwood pack sends an escort to meet surveyors at its gate (customary). Cass carries a sealed Registrar's letter for the escort.
- ch1: Cass's satchel: Office copy of the Highwood line, June's Office field notes, letter of commission, sealed letter for the escort, red-edged oath card.
- ch1: June chained Cairns One to Eight in four days; her Office notes stop at Cairn Nine.
- ch1: The Office index lists June's notes as 41 pages; only 40 are in the file. Page 41 is missing.
- ch1: Cass told Voss she measures "accurately", not generously.
