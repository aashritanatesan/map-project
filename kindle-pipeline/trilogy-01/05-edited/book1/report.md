### Repetition (about 8 edits)
- "Mara stops breathing" appeared in ch5 and ch14: varied in ch5.
- "Like a lamp" images: cut two (ch4 "two coals", ch5 "window catching the sun"). The dimmed-lamp motif for open ground (ch8, ch27) and the tell "like a lamp" are kept on purpose.
- "I don't say anything. There isn't anything." appeared twice word for word (ch4, ch17), and "I don't say anything. I don't think she wants me to." twice (ch5, ch27): varied the second of each, plus one in ch18.
- Ch11: two Mara speeches ran back to back with no beat between them. Added a beat.
- Counts after both rounds: "very still" 6, "for a long moment" 7, "I don't say anything" 7, "like a lamp" 6.

### Pacing
- No chapter sags badly. The middle (chapters 10 to 17) is the strongest stretch.
- The slowest are **ch18** (a quiet transition back to the line) and **ch22** (the morning in the cave). Both work as breathers after big set pieces, so I didn't cut them. If you want the book tighter, ch22's wrist-wrapping scene is the most trimmable (about 300 words).
- Chapters sit close to the 2,000-word floor (the shortest is 2,001, the longest 2,493). The book never feels rushed, but there's room to deepen chapters if a reader complains about length.

### Voice drift
None significant. First person, present tense, one narrator, warm and wry with real stakes, throughout. Cass's humour stays tied to her nerves and precision, as the voice sample asked.

## Still bothering me (your call, not mine)
1. **The "best thing I've ever..." running joke (6 uses).** It pays off in ch22 and ch28, but some readers may find it cute rather than charming. Consider cutting one or two of the early ones (ch4, ch7).
2. **June's witness statement (ch28).** It says she "observed" the bond, but she was never near Cass and Mara; she must have heard it from the Hollow elder. Book 2 should explain this, or the cliffhanger will feel like a cheat.
3. **How did Voss know where to send the ravine attacker?** It's implied that River scouts reported their position (ch3 to ch5), but it's never confirmed. Worth one line in Book 2.
4. **Heat placement.** The only on-page scene is ch21, at 76% of the book. That suits slow-burn readers. Romance readers sometimes expect the first one closer to 60 to 65%.
5. **Fact-check before publishing:** surveying terms (Gunter's chain: 66 ft, 100 links, about 7.92 in per link; theodolite; "closing the triangle") are used correctly. There's no real-world IP overlap that I could find, but run your own check on the title *Chain and Cairn* and the series name *Moonbound*.

None of these looks like an outline problem.
